About

Matthew McKay, Ph.D.

I’m a learning and data leader. I make learning systems and workforce data trustworthy, then turn them into decisions leaders act on.

My work sits where learning platforms and data meet. On the platform side, I’ve owned enterprise learning platforms and the operations that run on them: administration, HR system integrations, request intake and service governance. On the data side, I build the measurement and governance that make the numbers hold up: written metric definitions, data-quality checks, reconciliation to source, and role-based access.

I’ve done this work at a national services company, in the technology division of a large bank, as a director in higher education, and through my own consulting practice. I also teach data and information technology courses at a community college. I use AI to speed up analysis, and I show the checks that make the results safe to use.

I’m looking for a senior manager or director role leading learning technology or learning operations, in an organization that treats learning and workforce data as business infrastructure. I’m also open to workforce analytics and data governance leadership. I’m based in Western New York and open to remote roles.

How I work has more on how I scope, build and hand off a project.
