Agency-to-staff conversion calculator

Estimates whether converting one agency nurse's steady hours into a staff position saves money over a chosen horizon, after hiring, orientation, the ramp to proficiency, and turnover. Defaults are 2025 national benchmarks and the assumptions used in case study C at Tessaly Health, a fictional health system (case study). LIB-26.

Method: 2,000 simulated runs of one position, day by day: recruiting lag, orientation, ramp, exits and refills, with agency covering every gap. Same model as case study C, but with steady demand instead of the daily pattern. It prices cost only; continuity, quality and the strain of overtime aren't in it.

Code is AI-assisted (Claude). Benchmarks: BLS Employer Costs for Employee Compensation (Dec 2024); SIA/NATHO Travel Nurse Benchmarking Survey (2025); NSI National Health Care Retention & RN Staffing Report (2026). Other defaults are stated assumptions.
